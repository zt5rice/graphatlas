# Teams

- **Search Team** — led by Engineering Manager Liam O'Brien. Owns the Merlin search
  service and query-time relevance. Team members: Ethan Brooks, Jordan Diaz.
- **Infrastructure Team** — led by Engineering Manager Nora Al-Sayed. Owns the Kepler
  on-prem deployment product and SRE duties. Team members: Sam Osei.
- **Data Engineering Team** — led by Director Sofia Rossi. Owns the Helios data warehouse
  and analytics pipelines. Team members: Maya Patel.
- **Platform Team** — led by Director David Kim. Owns developer platform and internal
  tooling, including the Project Atlas knowledge platform.
- **Design Team** — led by Design Lead Hannah Lee. Supports product and customer portal work.
- **Product Management** — Senior PM Oliver Wright coordinates the Q3 roadmap.

People Operations and Finance are staff functions outside these product teams.
