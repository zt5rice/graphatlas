# Security Policy

- **Access tiers**: read-only, contributor, admin, production.
- **Production access** requires approval from David Kim (Director of Platform Engineering).
- **Customer data** access is logged and reviewed monthly by Grace Liu.
- **On-prem (Kepler) installations** must be patched within 7 days of a security release.

Violations are reported through the on-call channel.
